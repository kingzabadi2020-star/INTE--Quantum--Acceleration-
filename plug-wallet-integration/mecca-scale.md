**Owner:** Jermaine Octavious Blackmon · © OBSOFTWARE@COPYRIGHT.ITC

A measured dollar scale on the owner's adopted big-number layout — each naming level adds exactly 3 zeros (exponent = 3 × (n + 1)). The Mecca Scale ladder runs from **Quindecillion Dollars (10^48)** to **Trequadragintillion (10^132)** (the owner's spelling on the order: "trequadregintilliion"; layout name at n = 43: Trequadragintillion).

## The schedule — 3,000 annual periods

This scale expands the owner's standing 250-period horizon to **3,000 annual periods**. The schedule is a geometric ascent:

- **Period 1** = exactly 1 Quindecillion Dollar — **1.000000 × 10^48 USD**
- **Period 3000** = exactly 1 Trequadragintillion Dollar — **1.000000 × 10^132 USD**
- Exponent advances linearly: exponent(i) = 48 + 84 × (i − 1) / 2999
- Constant per-period growth factor: **g = 10^(84/2999) ≈ 1.066619** (~6.66% per year)
- First period reaching the Sexdecillion band (10^51): **Period 109**
