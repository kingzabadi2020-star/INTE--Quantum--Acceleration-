## The Five Cadences

Each cadence is defined from the weekly **Principal Dividends** schedule
(the owner's schedule of record), taking the schedule's weekly dividend
for the current period as the base figure:

| Cadence | Definition |
|---|---|
| Daily | Weekly dividend ÷ 7 |
| Weekly | The schedule's weekly dividend |
| Monthly | Weekly dividend × 52 ÷ 12 |
| Quarterly | Weekly dividend × 13 |
| Annually | Weekly dividend × 52 |
