## The Rule of the Register

- Every AI bot division, canister, and terminal/surface of the INTE system carries **one Spaceforce name** — its designation of record within his system.
- The individual bots are **numbered within their named division**: the register's named divisions span the fleet at **galactic scale — 830,000,000,000 bots**, the galactic scale of record (owner's order, 2026-10-31), which governs at galactic scale; the initial designation of **830,000,000** bots stands in the record as the fleet's first figure. The register names the divisions, the fleet, the canisters, and the surfaces — it does not list individual bots by name. **Operations currency at galactic scale: Light.**
- These names are designations of record within the owner's own system; they change nothing in the systems they name (each bot division, canister, and surface keeps its function, ticker, and status exactly as recorded elsewhere).
