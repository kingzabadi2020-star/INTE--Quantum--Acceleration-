## The Protocol — INTE Link Protocol

The INTE Link Protocol is the owner's protocol blueprint for mobile and local WiFi computing: his own devices, his own network design, and his own broadcasting services, built to run without depending on outside company software.

### Three layers, as designed

**1. Mobile Link**
Phones are first-class nodes of the protocol. A phone under this layer joins as a full participant — it can hold identity, receive broadcasts, and relay within the local design — not as a thin client of someone else's platform.

**2. Local WiFi Layer**
The local-network layer: relay and compute between the owner's devices over local WiFi. Devices on the owner's local network discover, relay, and compute with each other under the protocol's rules, independent of outside company software.

**3. Computer Nodes**
Local computers form the fixed layer of the protocol — the standing nodes that anchor the local network, hold the heavier compute, and keep the design's records.
