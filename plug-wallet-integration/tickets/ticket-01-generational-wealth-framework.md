## 2. Structural analysis of the wealth gap

The generational wealth gap represents the cumulative disparity in owned assets,
intellectual property, and equity across demographics. The primary systemic causes
that push future generations into poverty include:

- **Asset Depletion & Inheritance Deficits** — lack of transferable home equity,
  business holdings, or capital reserves across family lines, forcing every
  generation to start from a zero financial baseline.
- **Predatory Debt & Financial Exclusion** — high-interest credit cycles, lack of
  commercial credit access, and institutional barriers that prevent asset
  acquisition and capital compounding.
- **Catastrophic Unforeseen Costs** — medical emergencies, sudden economic
  displacement, and legal friction that rapidly exhaust liquid savings due to
  missing institutional safety nets.
- **Omission of Sovereign Portfolio Assets** — failure to register, structure, and
  monetize sovereign individual portfolios, intellectual property, patents, and
  brand assets at birth.

Every individual is recognized as a sovereign entity with innate portfolio capacity.
